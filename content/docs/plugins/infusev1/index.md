A remake of the **ORIGINAL** Infuse SMP plugin.  Not the revamped version we know today.  
It'll be available as a paper plugin and fabric mod.  

When you kill someone, you gain a positive effect, and they gain a negative effect.  
If you kill somone while you have a negative effect, one of them is removed.  
If you die while you have a positive effect, you lose one of them.  

InfuseV1 features one [custom item](/infusev1/items) that can strengthen your abilities.  
There are plenty of configurations for the plugin, down to being able to select what effects are considered positive and negative.  

This was a fun plugin to remake and I hope you have fun using it in your server.
