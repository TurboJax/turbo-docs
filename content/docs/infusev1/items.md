These items are fairly simple to use and easy to understand.

## Infuse Effect
First up is the **Infuse Effect**.  It's a grayish-white potion  
When you drink it, it gives you a random positive effect.  
If you have any negative effects, it removes one of them instead.

![infuse_effect_recipe](/img/infusev1/infuse_effect_recipe.png)

## Enhancer
Next up is the **Enhancer**.  It's a dark purple potion.  
It increases the level of your positive effects for a short time after you drink it.  
The duration of the enhancer is configurable, see [here](/infusev1/configuration#enhancer-configs).  The default time is 90 seconds.  

Recipe:
![enhancer_recipe](/img/infusev1/enhancer_recipe.png)

## Reviver
Finally we have the reviver.  It is a nether star.  
Right-clicking it opens up a menu with a list of every banned player.  Click on their head to revive them.  
The two feathers move you 1 page to the left/right.  
There are a lot of configs related to reviving people.  See [here](/infusev1/configuration#reviver-configs) for what they are.
